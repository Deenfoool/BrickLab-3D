PARTS-6 FIT QA

Assembled mechanical fit

This page is intentionally about how the final models look when they are actually mated, not how good they look as isolated catalog thumbnails.

Pin ↔ 3L beam
Open split pin must visibly enter the true 4.8 mm-class liftarm bore without floating collars or blocked holes.
Axle ↔ 20T gear ↔ bush
The nominal cross axle, keyed gear bore and bush should read as one compatible shaft family.
Knuckle ↔ hub ↔ road wheel
Bearing, hub axle and wheel bore should share one axis and believable seating depth.
Knuckle steering arm ↔ tie rod
The eye must sit around the semantic steering pin as a real hinge, not a floating snap point.
Reject on: visible gaps at nominal interfaces, impossible overlaps, blocked bores, z-fighting, duplicate port geometry, or any decorative detail that looks larger than the actual part.
