PARTS-6 REALISM QA

BrickLab high-fidelity part gallery

Review molded proportions and, most importantly, whether every visible mating port agrees with the real mechanical connector: pin, pin-hole, axle or axle-hole.

Hero fidelity

Wheels · bevel gears · Cardan/CV · gearbox · open differential are the current hero pass. Look for believable tyre carcasses, family-specific tread, recessed rims, tapered bevel teeth, proper bearing caps, split castings and an open differential carrier.

Hero micro detail

Tyre mold seam/vent nibs · rim witnesses · bevel face relief · Cardan snap rings · CV cage windows · gearbox service plugs/fasteners · differential axle seals/spider retainers. These are close-range manufacturing cues only and must remain collider-independent.

Fine mechanical detail

Steering · hubs · shocks · suspension · bearing block · motor · worm drive · sensors · connectors now include bearing races/seals, retaining rings, spring-seat lips, preload threads, dust-boot ribs, molded reinforcement, motor endbell/vents/cable gland, worm bearing supports and realistic case finish. These additions must never enlarge physics colliders.
